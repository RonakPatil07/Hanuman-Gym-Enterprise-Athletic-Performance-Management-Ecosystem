Our Heritage

Forging Physical Excellence Since 2018

Named after the timeless embodiment of strength, perseverance, and devotion, Hanuman Gym began in Ambernath with a single lifting platform and a radical commitment: to eliminate commercial fluff and bring real athletic training to lifters of all levels.

We believe progress isn't accidental. It is engineered through progressive overload, intelligent recovery, and macro-level nutritional precision. Whether you are stepping into a gym for the first time or preparing for a national powerlifting meet, Hanuman Gym provides the environment where champions are forged.

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our gym history, coaching protocols, and multi-branch access.

Do I get access to all 5 gym branches with one membership?

Yes! All Hanuman Gym standard, Pro Athlete, and Elite tier passes grant seamless 24/7 keyless biometric access across all 5 branches in Ambernath, Dadar, Thane, Kalyan, and Karjat.

How does Hanuman Gym differ from commercial chain gyms?

We eliminate crowded waiting lines and commercial gimmicks by maintaining calibrated competition plates, dedicated Olympic lifting platforms, certified CSCS coaches on the floor, and free thermal Finnish saunas.

Are personal training sessions included with memberships?

All members receive a complimentary initial biometric assessment and orientation. Pro Athlete members receive monthly form checkups, while Elite Tier members receive 4 dedicated 1-on-1 personal coaching sessions every month.

Can beginners join or is it only for advanced lifters?

Over 40% of our members start as beginners. Our coaches guide you step-by-step through proper lifting mechanics, injury prevention, and nutrition fundamentals in a welcoming, ego-free community.
