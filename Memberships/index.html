Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about membership tiers, facilities, and billing policies.

Can I freeze or pause my membership if I travel?

Yes! Pro Athlete and Elite Tier members can freeze their membership for up to 60 days per year at zero extra charge through the My Space dashboard or front desk.

Are there any hidden joining or registration fees?

Zero hidden fees. What you see on this page is the total price. Your membership includes all listed facility access, locker rooms, and mobile tracking apps.

How do the 1-on-1 personal trainer sessions work?

Elite Tier members receive 4 dedicated personal training sessions every month. You can choose any master coach and book private 60-minute slots directly online.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my tier at any time?

Yes, tier changes can be requested at any point during your billing cycle. Upgrades take effect immediately with pro-rated billing.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

All new memberships come with a 7-day money-back guarantee. For month-to-month plans, cancel whenever you wish prior to your next billing renewal.

How does the Refer and Earn program work?

Every active member gets a unique referral link. When a friend joins any paid plan using your link, you receive Rs.500 wallet credit automatically applied to your next billing cycle.
