Scientific Dynamic Warm-Up

5-Minute Injury Prevention Primer
1. Joint Lubrication & Thoracic Mobility

Cat-Cow (10 reps) + World's Greatest Stretch (5 reps/side) to mobilize the hips and thoracic spine.

2. Rotator Cuff & Upper Back Activation

Resistance Band Pull-Aparts (15 reps) + Band Over-and-Backs (10 reps) to seat the glenohumeral joint.

3. Hip & Glute Priming (For Squats/Deadlifts)

90/90 Hip Flow (6 reps/side) + Bodyweight Glute Bridges (15 reps with 2s squeeze).

4. Barbell Ramp-Up Sets (Non-Fatiguing)

Empty Bar x 10 reps → 50% Working Weight x 5 → 70% x 3 → 85% x 1 → Begin Working Sets.
