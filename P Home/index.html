/// FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about joining and training at Hanuman Gym.

What should I bring on my first workout day?
Please bring comfortable athletic attire, clean training shoes, a sweat towel, and a water bottle. Lockers and shower facilities are provided on-site.
Is Hanuman Gym suitable for absolute beginners?
Yes, absolutely! Every new member receives a complimentary 1-on-1 orientation and posture assessment with a certified coach to guide you through equipment setup and basic workout splits.
Can I access multiple branch locations with one membership?
Yes! Standard and Premium membership tiers grant seamless 24/7 access across all 10+ Hanuman Gym locations in Mumbai and Pune.
Are diet and nutrition prescriptions included?
Yes. All members have access to custom macro recipes in the portal, while Standard and Premium members receive coach-prescribed calorie and water targets synced directly to their My Space dashboard.
Can I pause or freeze my membership if I travel?
Yes, memberships can be placed on hold for up to 30 days per annual subscription with zero penalty fees. Simply inform our front desk or message us through the portal.
